Email Summarizer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Email Summarizer is a private, personal tool used only by its owner, Daniel Hernandez. It is not offered to the public.

The app uses read-only access to its owner's own Gmail account (ai.read.emails@gmail.com) to create short summaries of incoming messages for the owner. It does not send, delete, or change any email.

No email content or personal information is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Summaries are kept only on the owner's own computers.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings (Third-party apps with account access).

Contact: ai.read.emails@gmail.com
